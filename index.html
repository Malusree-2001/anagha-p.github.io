About Me

Crafting AI that doesn’t just think, it feels.

I’m a Master’s student in Computer Science who designs intelligent systems that move in real time and adapt like instinct. My work blends machine learning, computer vision, and creative interfaces to turn abstract data into deeply human experiences.

From a calorie-aware food recognition model powered by CNNs, to a music genre classifier that hears mood through MFCCs, I build AI that understands context, not just input.

At Stellantis, I helped develop digital instrument clusters using Unity and Qt, fusing engineering with aesthetic clarity inside the vehicle’s most human interface — the dashboard.

But my favorite tools? Curiosity. Emotion. Imagination. I sketch, I rewatch anime, I write prompts that push AI past the expected. My creative instincts don’t compete with my code — they guide it.

I believe the future of AI lies where logic meets empathy, where real-time meets resonance. That’s the world I’m here to build.
