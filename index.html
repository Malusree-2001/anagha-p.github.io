About Me

Passionate about building AI that responds in real time and resonates with people — not just machines. Blending machine learning, computer vision, and creative technology, I love crafting systems that feel intuitive, human-aware, and a little magical.

Currently pursuing a Master’s in Computer Science, I’ve worked on everything from real-time food recognition with CNNs to music genre classification using deep audio features. My approach combines strong model development with thoughtful UX — the kind that makes AI not just smart, but sensibly interactive.

Previously at Stellantis, I contributed to the development of digital instrument clusters using Unity and Qt, working at the intersection of automotive tech, UI design, and embedded systems.

When I’m not coding or experimenting with prompts, I’m probably sketching, rewatching anime, or dreaming up the next AI-powered side project.
